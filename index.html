About me

I am Tianyi Gao, an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Hongchao Qin.

My research interests include agent memory, graph data mining, database systems, and agentic search. I am interested in building reliable memory and retrieval systems for agents, especially where graph structure, temporal data, and evidence grounding matter. I believe that the emergent intelligence of AI can better help people discover the value of being human.

Feel free to reach out about internship opportunities, research collaborations, exchanging ideas, or simply making a new friend. 😊

Research interests

  • Agent Memory & Agent Harness DesignEvidence-admissible, temporal, and graph-structured memory mechanisms, together with the context, tool, and execution harnesses that make agents reliable.
  • Graph Data Mining & Database SystemsEfficient query processing and mining over temporal and attributed graph data.
  • Agentic SearchRetrieval, active knowledge acquisition, and search strategies for robust agent reasoning.
